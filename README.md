### 📡 Key Research & Systems Featured

* **[Web-TEMPEST](https://github.com/TA1EEI/vhf-morse-transmitter-monitor):** In-browser VHF RF transmission exploiting display pixel clock EMI leakage. Featured in the **UK NCSC CTO Weekly Briefing**, Hackaday, and RTL-SDR.com. ([Zenodo Archive: 10.5281/zenodo.22307838](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22307838))
* **[Class-D RF Covert Transmitter](https://github.com/TA1EEI/ClassD-VHF-Transmitter):** Physical-layer covert air-gap exfiltration via smartphone audio amplifier PWM harmonics. Demodulated on unmodified handheld radios. ([Zenodo Archive: 10.5281/zenodo.23082466](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23082466))
* **[HamDMR Desktop Client](https://github.com/TA1EEI/HamDMR):** Modernized, standalone C++ / Qt 6 client for digital voice radio networks with native APRS-IS integration.
* **Firmware Distributions:** Dedicated Turkish localized firmware builds and tactical modifications for Quansheng transceivers ([Egzumer](https://github.com/TA1EEI/egzumer-turkce) & [F4HWN](https://github.com/TA1EEI/f4hwn-uvk5-turkce)).
